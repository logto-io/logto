<Step index={props.index} title="Verify email domains and bind them to the SSO connector">

Provide the email `domains` of your organization on the connector `SSO Experience` tab. Unlike tenant-level enterprise SSO, each domain must pass an ownership check before it can be used for Console SSO:

1. Make sure the `Connection` settings are completed and saved. A domain can't be bound until the connector is configured.
2. Enter your email domain (e.g. `yourcompany.com`) and click `Add domain`. Logto generates a DNS `TXT` record for the domain.
3. Add the `TXT` record to the DNS provider of your domain. Logto checks the record automatically, and DNS changes may take some time to take effect.
4. Once the domain ownership is verified, Logto binds the domain to the SSO connector automatically and its status changes to `Bound`.

After the domain is bound, users signing in to Logto Console with email addresses in that domain will be guided to your identity provider for single sign-on.

</Step>
